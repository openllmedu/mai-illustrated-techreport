Inference on Custom Silicon

As reasoning models generate very long outputs (up to 128k tokens of thinking), inference cost and latency become critical deployment constraints. The team implemented MAI-Thinking-1 on Microsoft's custom MAIA-200Microsoft Azure Integrated Accelerator — a custom AI chip designed by Microsoft specifically for inference workloads. Unlike general-purpose GPUs, MAIA is optimized for the specific computation patterns of large language model serving.Learn more hardware.

40%+
Higher token throughput vs GB200 (same power budget)

The improvement is measured in performance per watt — not just raw speed but how many tokens you can generate for a given amount of electrical power. This matters enormously at datacenter scale where power is often the binding constraint.

Why performance per watt matters: A datacenter has a fixed power budget (say, 100 megawatts). With GB200 GPUs, you can serve X users simultaneously. With MAIA-200 at 40%+ better efficiency, you can serve 1.4X users from the same power budget — or serve the same users at lower electricity cost.

Inference Serving at Scale

Serving a ~1T parameter MoE model to production traffic requires solving several engineering challenges:

  • Model distribution: The full model doesn't fit on one GPU. Tensor parallelismSplitting each layer's weight matrices across multiple GPUs. Every GPU computes a portion of every layer, then synchronizes via all-reduce after each layer. Requires high-bandwidth interconnect (NVLink).Learn more splits weight matrices so each GPU holds a fraction; pipeline parallelismAssigning groups of consecutive layers to different GPUs. Data flows through them sequentially. Only activations (small) are communicated between stages. Can use slower interconnects than tensor parallelism.Learn more puts different layers on different GPUs.
  • Continuous batchingIteration-level scheduling where finished sequences are immediately replaced by new ones, rather than waiting for an entire batch to complete. Eliminates padding waste and achieves 2-3x higher throughput than static batching.Learn more: As soon as one request finishes generating, a new one takes its slot immediately. No padding waste, no waiting. Used by SGLang (MAI's inference engine), vLLM, and TensorRT-LLM.
  • KV cache management: Each concurrent request's KV cache consumes GPU memory. For long-context requests (128K+ tokens), the cache alone can exceed the model weights in memory. PagedAttentionA memory management technique that allocates KV cache in fixed-size pages (like virtual memory in operating systems) rather than contiguous blocks. Eliminates memory fragmentation and enables efficient memory sharing across requests.Learn more manages this with virtual memory-style page allocation.
  • Prefix caching: For agentic workloads where multiple turns share the same context prefix, caching the computed KV values for the shared portion avoids redundant computation. MAI achieves 97-98% prefix cache hit rates on multi-turn workloads.

Sustainability

MAI-Thinking-1 was primarily trained on Microsoft-owned infrastructure in Phoenix, AZ, built to LEED GoldLeadership in Energy and Environmental Design — a green building certification system. Gold is the second-highest tier (after Platinum). It recognizes buildings with excellent environmental sustainability, energy efficiency, water conservation, and indoor air quality.Learn more certification standards.

Environmental Commitments

  • 100% renewable energy match — In 2025, Microsoft matched 100% of its global electricity consumption with renewable energy
  • Renewable diesel — Backup generators use a renewable diesel that reduces net carbon emissions vs. petroleum diesel
  • Water positive commitment — More than $50M invested in public water assets, including a municipal water storage facility, pipeline extensions, and groundwater recharge programs

Community Investment

Microsoft's Community-First AI Infrastructure initiative focuses on minimizing environmental impact and investing in the communities where infrastructure is built:

  • Water infrastructure — Municipal water storage facility with the City of Goodyear, wastewater pipeline extensions, and groundwater recharge partnerships with the Gila River Water Storage system and The Nature Conservancy
  • Workforce development — Datacenter Academy programs at Estrella Mountain Community College and Glendale Community College, providing local residents with skills for infrastructure careers
Building frontier AI responsibly means addressing environmental impact at every level: renewable energy for training, custom silicon for efficient inference, and community partnerships where infrastructure is located.
