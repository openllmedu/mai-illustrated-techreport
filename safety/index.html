Why Red Teaming?

Automated safety benchmarks test known attack patterns. But adversaries are creative and adaptive — they find novel attack vectors that benchmarks don't cover. Red teamingThe practice of simulating adversarial attacks against a system to discover vulnerabilities. In AI safety, red teamers try to make the model produce harmful outputs through creative prompting strategies. Unlike automated benchmarks, human red teamers can adapt their strategies in real-time based on the model's responses.Learn more uses skilled human attackers to find these gaps and feed findings back into training.
